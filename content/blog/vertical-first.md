Most brands now need both a hero film and a feed full of vertical cuts. Reframing a 16:9 master after the fact almost always feels like a compromise.

## Frame for the center third

I protect a 9:16 safe zone in the monitor and block action inside it. Wides still breathe; verticals never lose a face.

## Shoot dedicated vertical moments

A quarter of the day goes to shots that only exist for the feed — hands, textures, first-person movement. Those clips are what stop the scroll.
