Shot over four foggy nights on the Northern California coast with a crew of nine. We leaned into practical light and natural haze, and graded toward a teal-and-amber palette that mirrors the story's push and pull between hope and grief.

- Official selection at three regional festivals
- Shot on a large-format cinema camera with vintage anamorphic glass
